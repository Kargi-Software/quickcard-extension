Ratings, cast & crew, awards, box office, and streaming links.

Four ways to search

Whichever fits the moment — QuickCard meets you where you already are.

Hover a recognized link

On certain sites (which depends on the topic — see Settings), hovering a link shows a card automatically.

Select text on any page

Select a title or name, click the small search icon that appears next to it.

Right-click a selection

Choose QuickCard → Search from the context menu.

Toolbar search bar

Click the icon and search.

Make it yours

A quick tour of what's in Settings — everything here can be turned on, off, or customized.

General

Topics

Turn any of the six topics on or off — a disabled topic stops appearing everywhere.

General

Turn features on or off

Hover cards, context-menu search, selection search, and Google suggestions in the toolbar bar — each has its own switch.

General

Card style

Choose Mini or Full as the default for hover and selection cards, and set the delay before the search icon appears.

Per topic

Info fields

Each topic has its own tab to choose exactly which fields and info show up on its card.

Per topic

Site links

Toggle links to streaming services, stores, and review sites individually, grouped by category — for each topic.

Per topic

Custom links

Add your own link buttons from a URL template — useful for any site not already covered.

Site Rules

Where it runs

Run QuickCard everywhere except a blacklist, or only on a whitelist — globally, and separately per topic.

Privacy

What stays local

Settings and site lists are stored only in your browser — never sent anywhere else.

You can reopen this anytime from the extension menu, or change anything later in Settings.

QuickCard doesn't collect personal information — settings stay in your browser only.
