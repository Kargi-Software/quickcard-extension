# QuickCard

**Install:** [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/quickcard/)

**About this extension**
QuickCard pulls information from several sites at once and shows it in one fast, compact card, with quick links to the related sites you actually use. Type a title or name in the toolbar, in your own language, and get the answer without opening a single tab.

**Multilingual search**

Search in Arabic, Russian, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Persian and many more languages. QuickCard translates your text first, shows you both the original and the translation, and lets you fix it if it's wrong. When a name has several matches, pick the right one from a short list.

**Fast, from many sources**

Each card combines data from multiple sources in one place, so you don't have to jump between sites. QuickCard only loads what you've turned on, which keeps cards quick.

**Quick links to related sites**

Jump straight to streaming services, stores, review sites, databases, social profiles and official pages. You can also add your own link buttons with URL templates.

**Six topics**

 **Movies & TV:** IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes style ratings, Metacritic scores, Cinemascore, cast and crew, awards, box office, episode lists, and a full Credits list for people with category filters
 **Games:** Metacritic scores, system requirements, and prices across Steam, PlayStation Store and Xbox / Microsoft Store, plus quick links to deal sites
 **Music:** artists, albums, tracks, lyrics, short previews and Grammy Awards
 **Football:** players, teams and coaches with Transfermarkt market value, transfers, honours and stats
 **Books:** book and author details, ratings, series and adaptations
 **Athletes:** country, team, medals, pro stats and recent games

**Built to be customized**

 Turn each topic on or off
 Every topic has its own settings tab where you choose exactly which info and links appear
 Choose a compact Mini card or a detailed Full card
 Run QuickCard on all sites except a blacklist, or only on a whitelist you define


Also works by selecting text on any page, from the right-click menu, and by hovering supported links.
